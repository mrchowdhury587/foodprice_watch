# How the system works

1. **Data (S22, S23).** 63 TCB Dhaka retail items, one row per item and report day, May 2021 onward. The 2022 sparse period is never imputed or scored. Features: price history (lags, deviations from the recent mean, volatility, days since the price last changed), calendar (Ramadan, Eid, month), weather (lagged 5 days), the onion export measure and the exchange rate, each joined on the date it became known.
2. **One model (S24-S26).** A single LightGBM predicts the log price change for 1, 7, 14 and 30 report days ahead for every item (horizon is a feature). Six candidates (tomorrow = today, ETS, ridge, random forest, LightGBM, LSTM) are compared on calendar 2024 only; the lowest error wins, ties go to the simpler model. Settings are tuned with Optuna on Jan-Sep 2024 and confirmed on Oct-Dec 2024.
3. **Sealed test (S27).** The settings are committed in `state/prereg_S27.yaml` before the 2025-01 to 2026-10 test is scored, once. Accuracy is reported against "tomorrow = today" by horizon, item group and item, with walk-forward and sliding-window checks and error analysis. If the model does not beat "tomorrow = today", the report says so.
4. **Ranges and alerts (S28).** The expected range of a forecast uses the item's last 120 forecast errors already known at the forecast date. An alert starts when the price is above the upper bound on 3 of the last 5 report days. The level is fixed from 2024 false alarms outside the event windows (`state/prereg_S28.yaml`), then the 12 shock episodes are scored once (detected or not, lead days, precision, placebo). Alerts are abnormal-rise detection, not early warning.
5. **Drivers (S29).** The model's additive contributions are summed by group (price history, calendar, weather, onion export measures, exchange rate). A rise the drivers do not account for is called "not explained by measured cost drivers".
6. **Export (S30), dashboard, update.** The production model is fitted on all data; three files feed the local dashboard `app/dashboard.py`. `tools/update.py` guides adding new prices.

Numbers for the report come only from `reports/project/tables/` and are listed in `reports/project/RESULTS_REGISTER.md`.
